## English

Cloud Droid Hub (蜚卓通) is built for cross-border e-commerce and international social media operations. It brings dedicated Android cloud phone environments to sellers, brands and teams working with TikTok, Facebook, WhatsApp and other apps used in global commerce.

From short-form video and brand promotion to customer inquiries and after-sales support, give your daily international operations a dedicated cloud phone workspace.

### Connect content, customers and global commerce

| Business scenario | Application focus |
| --- | --- |
| TikTok content operations | Short-form video, product showcases and brand storytelling |
| Facebook brand promotion | Brand pages, community engagement and campaign promotion |
| WhatsApp customer communication | Customer inquiries, pre-sales conversations and after-sales support |

### Dedicated cloud phones for everyday operations

- **Dedicated workspaces**: Organize cloud phone instances by store, brand or project, with separate applications and local data.
- **Centralized device management**: Deploy and manage cloud phones in one place, reducing the need to switch between physical devices.
- **Flexible deployment**: Build an environment that fits your own infrastructure and the scale of your operations.
- **Ongoing app compatibility work**: Improve compatibility with apps used in international commerce, guided by everyday operational needs.

### Keep your team focused on business

Whether you are a cross-border seller, an international brand, a social media team or a customer support team, Cloud Droid Hub starts with practical workflows and continuously refines the cloud phone experience.

**Cloud Droid Hub. Dedicated cloud phones for your global business.**
